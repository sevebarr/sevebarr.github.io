Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

Blackjack Trainer ("the App") is developed by Seve Barrameda. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information — including your game settings, session history, and accuracy statistics — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage and is never sent to any server.

No Accounts Required

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use any feature.

No Third-Party Tracking

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

Crash Reporting

The App uses Sentry for crash reporting. If the App crashes, an anonymous crash report may be sent to Sentry containing technical information about the error (such as a stack trace and device type). This report does not include any personally identifiable information.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from users of any age. It is safe for use by children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at milly.assists@gmail.com.
